Tailor Management System

A comprehensive Flutter-based Tailor Management System designed to digitize and streamline tailoring business operations. The application enables multiple tailors to manage customers, measurements, orders, and business records efficiently through a modern and user-friendly mobile interface.

Overview

Traditional tailoring businesses often rely on paper records for customer measurements, order details, and delivery schedules, which can lead to data loss, inefficiency, and management challenges. This application provides a digital solution that helps tailors organize their work, maintain customer records, and track orders effectively.

The system supports multiple tailor accounts, ensuring that each tailor can securely manage their own customers, measurements, and orders independently.
